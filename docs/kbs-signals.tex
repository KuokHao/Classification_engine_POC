\section*{How signals work}

The KBS stores all evidence as \textbf{signals} in working memory. Each signal has:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{name} --- unique identifier
  \item \texttt{group} --- category (identity, scam, phishing, \ldots)
  \item \texttt{strength} --- confidence from 0 to 1
  \item \texttt{value} --- optional payload (boolean, string, \ldots)
  \item \texttt{evidence} --- short human-readable reason
\end{itemize}

Signals arrive in two stages:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Initial signals} --- asserted from tool outputs via \texttt{factExtractor.js} before rules run.
  \item \textbf{Derived signals} --- asserted by rules in \texttt{kbs.js} when conditions match. These are hypotheses used by later rules and for scoring.
\end{enumerate}

Convention: a signal is asserted only when present / meaningful. Absence means ``not detected.''

\section{Classifications --- how signals become labels}

Each abuse type has a \textbf{threshold}. Rules add small score deltas when their
conditions match. When the cumulative score reaches the threshold, that
classification is \textbf{promoted}.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Classification} & \textbf{Threshold} \\
  \midrule
  Official & $0.7$ \\
  Impersonation & $0.6$ \\
  Phishing & $0.5$ \\
  Scam & $0.5$ \\
  Fake\_Shop & $0.45$ \\
  Recruitment\_Fraud & $0.5$ \\
  Gambling & $0.6$ \\
  Pornography & $0.6$ \\
  Parking\_Site & $0.4$ \\
  Other\_Site & $0.4$ \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip
\noindent
Decision order: (1)~Official only on \signal{trusted_infra};
(2)~brand-independent content (Gambling / Pornography / Parking);
(3)~brand-dependent threats --- skipped when \signal{trusted_infra};
(4)~Other\_Site residual if nothing else promoted.

Below, for each classification: \textbf{when it runs}, \textbf{gate},
\textbf{signals that matter}, and a short \textbf{promote path}.

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Official}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{official}, only if \signal{trusted_infra}.
  \item[Gate] Heuristic trust score $\geq 60$ (structurally healthy host).
  \item[Promote at] $0.7$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:}
\signal{official_brand_identity},
\signal{official_brand_logo_claim},
\signal{official_brand_name_claim},
\signal{official_brand_markup_claim}.

\textbf{Input signals that score Official:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Strong: \signal{brand_tls_org_match} (+ enterprise TLS),
        \signal{brand_registrant_org_match} (+ enterprise TLS or established domain).
  \item Weaker priors (alone cannot promote): \signal{brand_image_detected},
        \signal{brand_name_present}, \signal{brand_in_markup}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Typical promote path:} trusted host + TLS/WHOIS org match brand fingerprint.

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Impersonation}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{brand\_dependent} (not on trusted infra).
  \item[Gate] \texttt{isImpersonationScorable}:
        \signal{impersonation_candidate} and not \signal{impersonation_exempt}.
  \item[Promote at] $0.6$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:}
\signal{impersonation_candidate} (any brand claim),
\signal{impersonation_exempt} (commentary or established third party),
\signal{visual_brand_claim},
\signal{deceptive_subdomain},
\signal{stolen_face_tls},
\signal{phishing_trap},
\signal{burner_corporation},
\signal{geographic_imposter},
\signal{recruitment_brand_impersonation}.

\textbf{Input / scoring cues:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Brand claim: \signal{brand_name_present}, \signal{brand_image_detected},
        \signal{brand_in_markup}.
  \item Identity traps: logo prior; subdomain + password/OTP/login language;
        TLS identity mismatch; brand + credential capture;
        young/short-term + hidden WHOIS; country mismatch / bulletproof host.
  \item Bleeds: brand + fee/reward/investment/support language
        (\signal{impersonation_scam_fee_trap});
        hollow recruiter portal + fee/ID trap;
        trust hollow (missing/error trust nav, copyright, dead links) when scorable.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Typical promote path:} brand logo/text off-domain + password form, or
subdomain + login capture (single strong rule can clear $0.6$).

\noindent
Impersonation is the only label that uses \signal{impersonation_candidate} and
\signal{impersonation_exempt}. Other brand-dependent abuse labels require the
same page brand claim through \texttt{isBrandAbuseScorable} helpers, without a
shared candidacy signal.

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Phishing}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{brand\_dependent}.
  \item[Gate] \texttt{isPhishingScorable}: watched brand on page
        (\signal{brand_name_present}, \signal{brand_image_detected}, or
        \signal{brand_in_markup}) via \texttt{isBrandAbuseScorable} /
        \texttt{hasBrandClaim}. Does \emph{not} use
        \signal{impersonation_candidate} or \signal{impersonation_exempt}.
        Trust hollow Phishing deltas also require a credential-capture cue or
        impersonation-scorable brand.
  \item[Promote at] $0.5$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:}
\signal{phishing_trap} (also scores Impersonation).

\textbf{Input signals that score Phishing:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Login capture: \signal{has_password_input}, \signal{has_otp_input},
        \signal{has_username_input}, credential-login / credential-capture language.
  \item Sensitive forms: \signal{has_financial_input}, \signal{has_identity_input},
        \signal{has_file_upload} (rule \texttt{phishing\_sensitive\_inputs}).
  \item Mechanics: autocomplete-off on password; external form submission;
        account-verification / password-reset / urgency+account lures.
  \item Soft priors: missing/error trust nav, excessive dead links (when capture
        or impersonation context exists).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Typical promote path:} password or OTP field present ($+0.4$) plus one more
cue, or brand phishing trap ($+0.4$ Phishing from the same rule).

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Scam (gated additive)}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{brand\_dependent}.
  \item[Gate] \texttt{isScamScorable}: brand claim
        (\texttt{isBrandAbuseScorable} / \texttt{hasBrandClaim}) and not
        \signal{scam_exempt}
        (set when \signal{established_domain} or \signal{has_enterprise_tls}).
        There is no shared \texttt{brand\_abuse\_candidate} signal.
  \item[Promote at] $0.5$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:}
\signal{scam_exempt} only (no composite ``hooks'').

Evidence is split into three buckets. A leaf scores only if another bucket is also present:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Bucket} & \textbf{Signals} \\
  \midrule
  Lure (weak) &
    investment / reward-grant / official-tone language;
    strong brand claim while impersonation-scorable \\
  Extraction &
    fee / support-payment / recruitment-fee language;
    \signal{offplatform_chat_contact};
    \signal{free_webmail_contact};
    \signal{conversion_destination_dead};
    \signal{excessive_dead_links} \\
  Risk &
    young / newly registered / short-term;
    hidden registrant; bulletproof; no email;
    missing/error trust nav; missing copyright \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\textbf{Gates:}
extraction needs risk;
risk needs extraction or lure;
lure needs risk or extraction.

\textbf{Also adds a little Scam score:} unrealistic shop discount; recruitment advance-fee trap.

\textbf{Typical promote path (no semantics required):}
WhatsApp/Telegram link + young domain + hidden WHOIS.

\subsection{Recruitment\_Fraud}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{brand\_dependent}.
  \item[Gate] \texttt{isScamScorable} (brand claim and same
        \signal{scam_exempt} exemption as Scam).
  \item[Promote at] $0.5$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:}
\signal{advance_fee_recruitment_trap},
\signal{excessive_identity_harvesting},
\signal{hollow_recruiter_portal},
\signal{recruitment_brand_impersonation}.

\textbf{Input signals:}

\textbf{Typical promote path:} recruitment language + fee language ($+0.35$ trap) clears threshold with the language prior.

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Gambling}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{brand\_independent} (runs even on trusted infra).
  \item[Gate] None (\signal{scam_exempt} does not apply).
  \item[Promote at] $0.6$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:} none.

\textbf{Input / scoring cues (language-first tiers):}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Definitive keywords (\signal{definitive_gambling_language}): $+0.7$ --- alone promotes.
  \item Semantic (\signal{semantic_gambling_language_detected}): $+0.65$ --- alone promotes.
  \item Strong keywords (\signal{strong_gambling_language}): $+0.45$, or $+0.65$ when
        two or more distinct strong matches.
  \item Weak keywords (\signal{weak_gambling_language}): $+0.15$ --- never alone.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Typical promote path:} \texttt{casino} / \texttt{live dealer} / \texttt{sportsbook}
in visible text (definitive), or multi-word semantic gambling language, or two strong
keyword hits (e.g.\ \texttt{NBA betting} + \texttt{online slots}).
Weak-only copy such as ``Limited Slots'' stays below threshold.

  \item[Gate] None.
  \item[Promote at] $0.6$

\textbf{Input signals:}
\signal{semantic_adult_content_language_detected}

\subsection{Parking\_Site}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] Phase \texttt{brand\_independent}.
  \item[Gate] Investigation opens when page text contains \texttt{domain}
        or \texttt{domain name} (\signal{parking_keywords_present} $\rightarrow$
        \signal{parking_candidate}). Scorers skipped if password input +
        credential-login language (live phishing portal wins over parking).
        Gate tokens alone do not score.
  \item[Promote at] $0.4$
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:}
\signal{parking_candidate}
(from \signal{parking_keywords_present}).

\textbf{Scoring (after gate):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item High-confidence: \signal{parking_candidate} +
        \signal{semantic_parking_language_detected} ($+0.7$ --- alone promotes).
  \item Infra: parked IP ($+0.45$), parked NS ($+0.3$).
  \item Buddies: no-email infrastructure/capability ($+0.2$);
        semantic transaction language ($+0.2$).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Typical promote path:} page mentions ``domain'' and semantic parking
language fires (e.g.\ ParkLogic ``available to be registered'').

\subsection{Other\_Site}

\begin{description}
  \item[When] After all phases, if nothing else promoted.
  \item[Gate] Residual fallback only.
  \item[Promote at] $0.4$ (engine adds $+1.0$)
\end{description}

\textbf{Derived facts created:} none.
No content/threat class matched.
